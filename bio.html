Professional Bio

Mustafa Serour

Product Owner · Business Analyst · MBA Candidate

Mustafa Serour is a Product Owner and Business Analyst with over a decade of professional experience spanning regulated financial services, fintech, HR tech, and healthcare-adjacent platforms. He brings a rare combination of deep domain expertise and structured product thinking — shaped by 7 years in banking and sharpened through 3 years of end-to-end SaaS product delivery.

Currently serving as Product Owner at Talento, a B2B HR tech SaaS platform serving the MENA region, Mustafa owns the full product lifecycle across three portals — Admin, Employer, and Employee. He has architected and shipped an EOR employee lifecycle system, a multi-tier invoicing engine with split-salary logic, and a role-based access control framework covering 10+ roles — all delivered from stakeholder interviews through to developer-ready specifications.

Prior to Talento, Mustafa worked as a Business Analyst at Tarmeez Capital, a MENA fintech investment platform, where structured gap analysis and process mapping contributed to a 15% operational efficiency improvement. Earlier, as Product Owner at Dawafast Co, he led four consecutive product releases on schedule — driving a measured 20% improvement in customer satisfaction.

Before transitioning into product, Mustafa spent seven years at QNB Al-Ahli Bank as a Relationship Manager — building the client management, financial analysis, and regulatory compliance foundations that now underpin his work in fintech and HR tech.

Mustafa holds a PSPO certification from Scrum.org and is currently completing an MBA in Strategic Management and Innovation at the German University in Cairo. He is fluent in Arabic and English, with basic Chinese, and is open to Product Owner, Product Manager, and Business Analyst roles.
